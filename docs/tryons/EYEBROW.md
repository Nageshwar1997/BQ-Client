# EYEBROW Try-On Tracker

[← Back to master tracker](./README.md) · [← Back to EYE category](./EYE.md) · [← Build plan](./EYE-PLAN.md)

_Tracking model: face landmarks (eyebrow ring, nose tip). Depends on the shared face-landmark engine — see [README.md](./README.md#shared-prerequisites-ye-pehle-banao--sabko-block-karte-hain)._

> **Per-subcategory tracker** — same convention [EYELINER.md](./EYELINER.md)/[KAJAL.md](./KAJAL.md)/[EYESHADOW.md](./EYESHADOW.md) established: as a subcategory gets built, it gets its own dedicated tracker file and [EYE.md](./EYE.md)'s own inline checklist for it gets replaced with a summary-row + link.

> **Ek real, directly-tracked closed region pe bani pehli EYE finish**: EYELINER/KAJAL ek open arc trace karte hain, EYESHADOW ek _synthesized_ region wash karta hai (lash line ka ek offset, kyunki koi crease landmark exist nahi karta) - EYEBROW pehli hai jahan MediaPipe already poori region ko directly ek closed ring ki tarah track karta hai (`LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES`, same points jo FACE ke apne full-face washes already apni exclusion holes ke liye use karte hain). 5 patterns: Bold/Defined Fill, Soft Powder Fill, aur Ombre Brow closed-region fills hain (flat, blurred, ya gradient); Natural Hair-Stroke aur Feathered/Fluffy iske bajaye region ke across kayi individual tapered strokes draw karte hain, ek hair-like texture ke liye - procedural canvas math deterministic per-stroke variation ke saath, ek texture image asset nahi.

## Design notes

- **Ek real closed ring, first/last-point ordering nahi**: `LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES` (10 points each) brow ke apne physical loop ke around continuously trace karte hain - open eye arcs ke ulat, jahan `orderInnerToOuter` decide karta hai ki kaunsa literal endpoint inner vs outer hai, ek ring ke apne first/last points bas jahan bhi index list loop ke around shuru/khatam hoti hai wahan hote hain, zaroori nahi ki koi anatomical end ho. `findInnerOuterPoints` iski jagah ring ke har point ko directly scan karta hai jo bhi nose tip se sabse kareeb (inner) aur sabse door (outer) baithta hai - correct chahe ring ke apne indices kaise bhi ordered hon, Ombre Brow ke apne gradient axis ke liye chahiye.
- **`traceEyebrowPath` - light rounded-corner trace, fully curve-smoothed nahi**: ring ke apne har point pe (10, ya 11 jab tail-landmark fix neeche land ho jaye), dono adjoining edges ke saath thoda sa pull back karta hai aur un do pulled-back points ko ek `quadraticCurveTo` se connect karta hai real vertex ke through - neeche wale fix bullets dekho poori history ke liye ki ye yahan kyun land hua (full curve-smoothing nahi, pure straight lines bhi nahi).
- **"Too curvy/rounded, real brow cover nahi karta" - commverse ke apne reference implementation ke against fix kiya**: directly ek real render ke against report hua - dono eyebrows ek uniformly round arch ki tarah fill hote the jo model ki apni actual (zyada angular, kam symmetric) eyebrow ke neeche track karne ki jagah ek generic idealized brow shape jaisa dikhta tha. `src/commverse` ka apna eyebrow rendering (`utils/index.ts`'s `clipLipsOnFace`/`drawEyebrow`) directly check kiya: ye same tarah ka raw landmark ring ko **plain straight lines** se trace karta hai (`moveTo`/`lineTo` per point, `closePath()`) aur kahin bhi smoothing step nahi. Hamara apna `traceEyebrowPath` iske bajaye ek quadratic-curve-through-midpoints pass chala raha tha (FACE ke closed regions se borrowed same technique) - FACE ke apne much denser landmark rings ke liye theek hai, lekin sirf 10 sparse eyebrow points pe wo "cut every corner" technique shape ko measurably ek rounder arch mein inflate kar deti hai, real, zyada angular brow silhouette jo sparse points actually describe karte hain usse. Fixed karke `traceEyebrowPath` ko commverse jo same plain straight-line trace use karta hai usme switch kiya - koi naye landmark indices nahi chahiye the (`LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES` unchanged), purely ek tracing-technique change. `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` (Natural Hair-Stroke/Feathered-Fluffy) untouched raha, kyunki ye already apne strokes directly ring ke raw points se banata hai, `traceEyebrowPath` se guzre bina. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against sab 5 patterns ke across verify kiya (plus BROWGEL, jo `fillEyebrowRegion` share karta hai) - brow shape ab real eyebrow ka ek faithful, angular trace jaisa read hota hai, ek rounded blob ki jagah, dono eyes pe.
- **"Abhi bhi poori eyebrow cover nahi ho rahi, especially ends" - straight-line change ke upar ek doosra, separate fix**: dobara report hua, directly ek real render ke against, upar wala straight-line fix land hone ke turant baad - straight lines ab _landmark ring ke apne_ shape ke faithful the, lekin ring khud abhi bhi is model ki real (fuller) brow ke andar visibly baitha tha, sabse zyada tail pe, jahan real hair ring ke last point ke paar taper hota hai. commverse ka apna extra tail-side landmark (`156` left / `383` right, `data/index.ts`) ek possible fix ki tarah check kiya, same tarah jaise EYELINER corner-fade fix ne commverse se extra landmarks borrow kiye the - lekin ek debug overlay (ring ke apne 10 points aur 156/383 dono ko directly ek real render pe draw karke) ne dikhaya ki 156/383 eye ke apne outer corner ki taraf _neeche_ baithte hain, brow ke apne taper ke saath aur _door_ nahi; inme se kisi ko bhi ring mein straight splice karna tail ko bare skin mein neeche khींch deta, sideways extend nahi karta - is specific symptom ke liye galat fix. Iski jagah `dilateAroundCentroid` add kiya (utils/tryon-utils/eye.ts): har ring point ko ring ke apne centroid se ek fixed `EYEBROW_REGION_DILATION_RATIO` (originally `0.16`, baad mein tune down kiya - neeche dekho) se outward scale karta hai - ek plain geometric margin, same "extra coverage directly synthesize karo" philosophy jaisa `renderCornerFadeTail`, bas ek closed region pe apply hua, ek stroke ke apne endpoint ki jagah. Ek baar apply hua, `toPoints` ke turant baad, dono `applyEyebrowEye` aur `applyBrowgelEye` mein - isliye ring ka har downstream use (fill boundary, `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` ka apna edge sampling, `findInnerOuterPoints` ke inner/outer picks, Ombre Brow ka gradient axis) same enlarged region consistently dekhta hai, ek change per entry point se. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against sab 5 EYEBROW patterns plus BROWGEL ke across verify kiya: tail aur top-arch dono ab poori tarah covered read hote hain, kisi bhi brow pe fill ke apne edge ke paar koi bare hair visible nahi.
- **"Middle 3 patterns bahut zyada bahar ja rahe hain, first aur last sahi hain" - ek tail-taper follow-up revert hua**: upar wale dilation fix ke baad, fill patterns ke apne tail corner pe abhi bhi ek hard "cut" visible tha, isliye ek follow-up change ne `traceEyebrowPathTapered` add kiya - fill boundary ke do edges ko tail pe apne local tangent ke saath extend karte hue, `renderCornerFadeTail` ko mirror karte hue. Wo change sirf `fillEyebrowRegion` ke apne trace call ko touch karta tha, `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` ko kabhi nahi - isliye Soft Powder Fill/Bold/Defined Fill/Ombre Brow (3 fill patterns, `TEyebrowPattern` ke apne declaration order mein Natural Hair-Stroke aur Feathered/Fluffy ke beech mein baithe hue) Natural Hair-Stroke aur Feathered/Fluffy (first aur last patterns, unaffected kyunki wo kabhi `traceEyebrowPathTapered` call nahi karte) se visibly zyada door pahunch gaye - directly report hua ki wo 3 doosre 2 ki apni correct position ke paar overshoot kar rahe hain, ek explicit instruction ke saath ki sab 5 ko hair-stroke patterns ke apne extent se match karo. `fillEyebrowRegion` ko plain dilated ring trace karne pe revert karke fix kiya (koi extra taper nahi) - same boundary jo `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` already apne edges sample karta hai usse - aur ab-unused `traceEyebrowPathTapered`/`extendAlongTangent`/`EYEBROW_TAIL_TAPER_LENGTH_RATIO` delete kiya. Sab 5 patterns ab exactly ek region share karte hain (dilated ring), isliye ab per-pattern position drift shuru se hi nahi hai. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against verify kiya: sab 5 patterns ka apna tail extent ab ek doosre ke saath pixel-for-pixel line up karta hai.
- **"Ends ki length bahut zyada hai, kam karo" - `EYEBROW_REGION_DILATION_RATIO` `0.16` se `0.08` tak reduce kiya**: sab 5 patterns dobara ek region share karne ke baad (previous fix), complaint _patterns ke beech mismatch_ se shift hoke _shared_ length pe aa gayi - `dilateAroundCentroid` har point ki distance ko ring ke apne centroid se scale karta hai, isliye center se pehle se hi farthest points (inner aur outer corners - brow ke apne "ends") center ke kareeb wale points se proportionally zyada absolute amount se move karte hain, ek uniform margin ki jagah visibly elongated tips jaisa dikhta hai. Directly confirm kiya: approach khud (uniform dilation, sab 5 patterns ise share karte hue) sahi thi, bas ratio bahut aggressive thi - redesign karne ki jagah halve karna simple tha. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against sab 5 patterns ke across verify kiya: ends ab ek natural, proportioned taper jaisa read hote hain, visibly stretched-out points ki jagah.
- **"Too curvy" phir se (is baar matlab jagged, rounded nahi) + "ends ko thoda sloppy banao" - straight-line trace ke upar light corner-rounding add kiya**: dilation ratio tune down hone ke baad, complaint teesri baar shift hui - _pure_ straight-line trace (earlier commverse-matching fix se) har 10 landmark points pe visibly kinked read hoti thi ek baar aap ek over/under-sized region se distract nahi ho rahe the, aur do real tail/inner-corner joints specifically ek unnaturally precise geometric point jaisi dikhte the, ek real brow ke apne soft taper ki jagah. `traceEyebrowPath` ab har vertex ko ek _small_ `EYEBROW_CORNER_ROUND_RATIO` (`0.18`) se round karta hai - dono adjoining edges ke saath us fraction se pull back karo aur do pulled-back points ko ek `quadraticCurveTo` se connect karo real vertex ke through, original (deleted) midpoint technique ke ulat jo hamesha har edge ka poora 50% round karti thi aur poori shape inflate kar deti thi. Ring ke apne do real corners (jahan upper aur lower edges actually milte hain - same tarah dhoondhe jaise tail-taper fix ne identify kiye the, har corner ki apni distance `findInnerOuterPoints`' `outer` se compare karke) ko ek separate, larger `EYEBROW_END_ROUND_RATIO` (`0.45`) milta hai wahan deliberately looser, softer taper ke liye. `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` abhi bhi `traceEyebrowPath` ko bilkul call nahi karta, isliye ye sirf 3 fill patterns ke apne boundary ko affect karta hai, is section ke har fix jaisi hi scoping. North Indian/Ruby Red photo (Bold/Defined Fill) ke against verify kiya: landmarks ke beech edges ab smoothly rounded read hote hain kinked ki jagah, aur dono tail ends ek soft, thoda loose point tak taper karte hain ek sharp geometric corner ki jagah.
- **"Commverse dobara dekho, uski position proper hai" - commverse ka real tail landmark adopt kiya, ek margin synthesize karte rehna ki jagah, phir ek follow-up request ear-side top ko aur loosen karne ke liye**: do synthetic fixes (`dilateAroundCentroid`, phir `traceEyebrowPathTapered`) us landmark data ke liye stand in kar rahe the jo humare paas nahi tha - is baar commverse ke apne `left_eyebrow_indices`/`right_eyebrow_indices` (`data/index.ts`) ko directly re-check kiya (sirf earlier `clipLipsOnFace` tracing-technique check nahi) aur paaya ki inme ek extra real tail-side point hai (`156` left / `383` right) jo kisi bhi synthetic fix se match nahi hua tha; commverse ka apna rendering tail ka correct extent purely us ek point se paata hai, upar koi extra margin ke bina. Same point `LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES` mein add kiya (constants/tryon-constants/eye.ts, har array ke end mein appended) aur dono synthetic fixes poori tarah remove kiye (`dilateAroundCentroid`/`EYEBROW_REGION_DILATION_RATIO`, `traceEyebrowPathTapered`/`extendAlongTangent`/`EYEBROW_TAIL_TAPER_LENGTH_RATIO`) - real landmark data ek geometric approximation se behtar hota hai. Naye point ko `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` ke apne ring se **bahar** rakha (`applyEyebrowForEye` ab `.slice(0, 10)` se ise hair-strokes ko pass karne se pehle nikal deta hai): wo point eye ke apne outer corner ki taraf neeche baithta hai, brow ki apni hair line ke saath nahi (ek earlier debug overlay se confirm kiya), isliye hair-stroke patterns apne edges original 10-point ring se hi sample karte rehte hain, jo already independently correct confirm hui thi aur poori tarah unaffected rahi. `traceEyebrowPath` khud ko bhi yahan ek rework chahiye tha: ek 11-point ring ke saath (10 + ek appended tail point) purana half-split corner detection (`half-1`/`half`, `n-1`/`0`) ab ring ke real do corners pe land nahi karta, isliye ab ye actual inner/outer vertices ko `points` ko `findInnerOuterPoints`' apne `inner`/`outer` (nearest-point search) ke against match karke dhoondhta hai, array-position symmetry assume karne ki jagah - kaam karta hai chahe ring mein kitne bhi points hon. Ek follow-up request ("ear-side end ko thoda aur sloppy banao, upar se") ne ek aur tier add kiya: outer point ka apna _upper_ neighbor (uske do adjoining vertices mein se jiska y chhota hai) ko ek aur bhi larger `EYEBROW_OUTER_TOP_ROUND_RATIO` (`0.7`) milta hai general `EYEBROW_END_ROUND_RATIO` ke upar. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against sab 5 patterns ke across verify kiya: tail ab ek soft point tak taper hota hai poore hair coverage ke saath aur koi hard cut nahi, commverse ke apne reference shape se match karte hue, jabki Natural Hair-Stroke/Feathered-Fluffy apne already-correct look se visually unchanged rahe.
- **Hair-strokes procedural hain, ek texture asset nahi**: `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` 10-point ring ko uske do edges mein split karta hai (first half/second half, second reversed taaki dono same rotational direction mein chalein), har ek ko ek dense point sequence mein smooth karta hai, phir `strokeCount` mein se har stroke ke liye ek single tapered segment draw karta hai (`fillTaperedPath`, exact same ribbon primitive jo har doosri stroke-based EYE finish already use karti hai) lower edge ke ek point se upper edge ke ek point tak - do edge points ka apna y render time pe compare karke (kaunsa constant array "upper" hai ye trust kiye bina) dhoondne ke liye ki asal mein hair kahan se base grow karta hai. `strokeJitter` (ek deterministic sine-based hash, `Math.random()` nahi) har stroke ke angle/length/width ko reproducibly vary karta hai, isliye same photo hamesha identically render hota hai aur is file ke apne smoke tests deterministic rehte hain. `strokeAngleBiasDeg` har stroke ki direction ko straight-up ki taraf blend karta hai - Natural Hair-Stroke ke apne "brow ki natural line ke saath lying" look ke liye 0, Feathered/Fluffy ke "brushed up" soap-brow look ke liye ek much larger value - dono patterns ke beech ka akela tuning difference.
- **Ombre Brow ka gradient axis ring ki apni inner→outer line hai, eye ki nahi**: `ctx.createLinearGradient(inner.x, inner.y, outer.x, outer.y)` eyebrow ring ke apne `findInnerOuterPoints` result ka use karke, front/inner end pe lighter (`mixTowardWhite`), tail/outer end pe bolder (`mixTowardBlack`) - same per-channel math jo EYESHADOW already FACE ke apne HIGHLIGHTER/CONTOUR se port kar chuka hai.
- **Pattern-preview icons**: 5 icons (`public/images/tryon/eye/eyebrow/*.webp`) same base template reuse karte hain (light-gray lid-crease arc, gray iris + white highlight, thin black lash-line boundary) jo har doosri EYE finish ka apna icon set use karta hai, ek one-off `canvas`-package Node script se generate kiye (commit nahi kiya, har doosri EYE finish ke apne icon generation jaisa hi temporary-tooling treatment), PNG→WebP `cwebp` se convert kiya. Ek proper brow-arch shape (apna quadratic curves ka pair, template ke apne decorative crease line se independent) eye ke upar baithta hai, per pattern styled - 3 fill patterns ke liye ek neutral brunette tone, baaki 2 ke liye individual procedural hair-strokes (exact same jitter/taper technique jo real renderer use karta hai, icon script ke liye reimplemented), jo icon scale pe bhi genuinely convincing individual-hair textures jaisa read hote hain.
- **Ab tak ki verification**: `tsc -b --force`, `eslint`, aur poori `vitest` suite (95/95, 7 naye `applyEyebrowEye` smoke tests samet - sab 5 patterns, ek unrecognized-pattern-id case, aur ek EYELINER-id-cross-contamination case) sab pass hoti hai. Real-photo browser verification (North Indian model, Berry Wine, sab 5 patterns): har pattern dono eyebrows pe real brow shape ko closely follow karte hue render hota hai, per pattern ek visibly distinct look ke saath (Soft Powder ka blur, Bold/Defined ke crisp edges, Ombre ka light-to-dark gradient, Natural Hair-Stroke ka fine strand texture model ke apne real brow hairs ke saath convincingly blend karta hai, Feathered/Fluffy ke zyada upward-brushed strokes) - aur Bold/Defined Fill aur Natural Hair-Stroke ke beech ek pixel-diff ne brow region mein 7,705 genuinely changed pixels confirm kiye.
