### Design notes

- **Ek real closed ring, first/last-point ordering nahi**: `LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES` (10 points each) brow ke apne physical loop ke around continuously trace karte hain - open eye arcs ke ulat, jahan `orderInnerToOuter` decide karta hai ki kaunsa literal endpoint inner vs outer hai, ek ring ke apne first/last points bas jahan bhi index list loop ke around shuru/khatam hoti hai wahan hote hain, zaroori nahi ki koi anatomical end ho. `findInnerOuterPoints` iski jagah ring ke har point ko directly scan karta hai jo bhi nose tip se sabse kareeb (inner) aur sabse door (outer) baithta hai - correct chahe ring ke apne indices kaise bhi ordered hon, Ombre Brow ke apne gradient axis ke liye chahiye.
- **`traceEyebrowPath` - light rounded-corner trace, fully curve-smoothed nahi**: ring ke apne har point pe (10, ya 11 jab tail-landmark fix neeche land ho jaye), dono adjoining edges ke saath thoda sa pull back karta hai aur un do pulled-back points ko ek `quadraticCurveTo` se connect karta hai real vertex ke through - neeche wale fix bullets dekho poori history ke liye ki ye yahan kyun land hua (full curve-smoothing nahi, pure straight lines bhi nahi).
- **"Too curvy/rounded, real brow cover nahi karta" - root-caused apne geometry se**: directly ek real render ke against report hua - dono eyebrows ek uniformly round arch ki tarah fill hote the jo model ki apni actual (zyada angular, kam symmetric) eyebrow ke neeche track karne ki jagah ek generic idealized brow shape jaisa dikhta tha. Root cause `traceEyebrowPath`'s apna quadratic-curve-through-midpoints pass nikla (FACE ke closed regions se borrowed same technique) - FACE ke apne much denser landmark rings ke liye theek hai, lekin sirf 10 sparse eyebrow points pe wo "cut every corner" technique shape ko measurably ek rounder arch mein inflate kar deti hai, real, zyada angular brow silhouette jo sparse points actually describe karte hain usse. Fixed karke `traceEyebrowPath` ko ek plain straight-line trace (`moveTo`/`lineTo` per point, `closePath()`, koi smoothing step nahi) mein switch kiya - koi naye landmark indices nahi chahiye the (`LEFT/RIGHT_EYEBROW_INDICES` unchanged), purely ek tracing-technique change. `renderEyebrowHairStrokes` (Natural Hair-Stroke/Feathered-Fluffy) untouched raha, kyunki ye already apne strokes directly ring ke raw points se banata hai, `traceEyebrowPath` se guzre bina. North Indian/Ruby Red photo ke against sab 5 patterns ke across verify kiya (plus BROWGEL, jo `fillEyebrowRegion` share karta hai) - brow shape ab real eyebrow ka ek faithful, angular trace jaisa read hota hai, ek rounded blob ki jagah, dono eyes pe.
- **"Abhi bhi poori eyebrow cover nahi ho rahi, especially ends" - straight-line change ke upar ek doosra, separate fix**: dobara report hua, directly ek real render ke against, upar wala straight-line fix land hone ke turant baad - straight lines ab _landmark ring ke apne_ shape ke faithful the, lekin ring khud abhi bhi is model ki real (fuller) brow ke andar visibly baitha tha, sabse zyada tail pe, jahan real hair ring ke last point ke paar taper hota hai. ### Design notes

- **Shared tapered-stroke primitive**: har pattern upper lash line ke saath (`LEFT_EYE_UPPER_INDICES`/`RIGHT_EYE_UPPER_INDICES`, `constants/tryon-constants/eye.ts`) ek continuous filled path hai, ek per-`t` width function (`lashToWidth`) ke saath - inner corner ke kareeb thin, outer ke kareeb thicker, optionally outer corner ke paar ek curved "wing" extension mein continue karta hua. Classic Thin/Bold Thick/Tightline is ek primitive pe bas alag base/peak width ratios hain; Winged/Double Wing wing add karte hain; Smokey/Smudged ek `ctx.filter` blur pass add karta hai (same technique jo LIP ka apna `LINER` finish already establish kar chuka tha); Underliner ek second pass add karta hai jo lower arc trace karta hai. Saare widths/lengths eye ke apne detected width (inner-to-outer corner distance) ke ratios hain, fixed pixels nahi - same "feature ke apne size se scale karo" convention jo is app ka har doosra placement constant follow karta hai.

  1. `orderInnerToOuter` - decide karta hai ki arc ka kaunsa end inner (nasal) corner hai, har end ke x-distance ko nose tip se compare karke render time pe, ek fixed index ko hamesha "inner" maanne ki jagah.
  2. `outwardNormalsAlongPath` - har path point pe width offset direction, _local path tangent_ se derive hui (har jagah correctly perpendicular, ek curving wing pe bhi, ek pure "centroid se door" direction ke ulat jo eye ke apne center se door "outward" ka matlab rakhna band kar deti hai). Tangent-perpendicular apne aap 180° se ambiguous hai har point pe, isliye sign ek baar decide hota hai path ke pehle point pe (centroid ke through) aur phir point-to-point continuity se propagate hota hai (har normal jo closest rahe, dot product se, pichle point ke apne chosen normal ke) - ek discrete parallel-transport, har point ko independently centroid ke against re-test karne ki jagah (iska ek earlier version, jo wing pe door apne neighbors se disagree kar sakta tha). Wing ki apni launch direction bhi similarly ek `horizontalSign` leti hai (eye ke apne inner→outer direction se derive hui) taaki ye dono sides pe nose se door sweep kare, ek fixed "hamesha rightward" angle nahi.
  3. **`fillTaperedPath`'s fill strategy**: har point pe correct normal direction hone ke baad bhi, ek single polygon jo poora offset edge ke saath bahar jaata hai aur path ke saath wapas aata hai abhi bhi ek classic offset-curve trap hai - jahan bhi path wahan lagaye gaye offset width se tighter bend karta hai (exactly jo ek wing ka sharp launch lash line se karta hai), outer edge khud ko cross karta hai, aur ek single self-crossing filled path visually disconnected lobes ki tarah render ho sakta hai ek gap ke saath. Ribbon ko chhote quads mein decompose karke fix kiya (ek per adjacent point pair), sab same rotational direction mein wound, saath mein ek `fill()` call mein filled - canvas ka nonzero winding rule phir kisi bhi quad-to-quad overlap ko solid rakhta hai, gap mein cancel karne ki jagah, aur abhi bhi ek `fill()` call hai isliye color ka apna alpha sirf ek baar blend hota hai.

1. **Single wash** - poore lid ke across ek flat color
2. **Two-tone gradient** - brow bone ke kareeb lighter, crease mein darker

4. **Cut crease** - crease pe ek sharp defined line, high-contrast
5. **Halo eye** - lid ka center light/shimmer, outer corners + crease pe dark

1. **Natural hair-stroke** - individual-hair-like texture (ek texture asset chahiye, LIP ka texture pipeline)
